## 4. How to Host Your Privacy Policy URL for Google Play Console

Google Play requires a public HTTPS URL for your privacy policy in **Policy and programs > App content > Privacy Policy**.

### Recommended Free 1-Minute Option: GitHub Pages
1. Go to [github.com](https://github.com) and create a public repository named `wireloop-policy`.
2. Add a file named `index.md` (or `index.html`) and paste the Privacy Policy markdown text above into it.
3. Go to **Settings > Pages > Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and click **Save**.
4. Within 30 seconds, your live HTTPS URL will be ready at:  
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/wireloop-policy/`
5. Paste that URL directly into your Google Play Console!
